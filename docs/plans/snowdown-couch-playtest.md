# Snowdown: five-minute couch playtest

Status: Eric reports friends love the prior polished demo. **The new charge mechanic and Moonwalk Marshal need their feedback**; no specific physical-controller hardware results were supplied. Automated gamepad simulation does not substitute for that.

Preview: https://game-preview.ninjaprivacy.org/games/snowdown/. Use a desktop browser with hardware acceleration, preferably on the shared display. Reload after a new build.

## Two humans, one screen

1. Choose **Ride together**. P1 uses WASD/F/G; P2 uses arrows/K/L. Both move and hold throw simultaneously. Check keyboard rollover.
2. Hold **H (P1)** or **J (P2)** to pack a Big Ol' Snowball; release to launch. Controllers use **X**. Try short and full charges.
3. Have your partner shoot the rolling ball with normal F/K/A/RT throws. Do you see the boost and the ball growing? Try bowling through several outlaws.
4. Walk/dodge in different directions. Up should look like up-screen. Separate: can both players find their cowboy and read the markers/cooldowns?
5. Let one partner freeze; stand beside them to revive. Then clear **three normal waves** and face the **Moonwalk Marshal**. Are the glove volley and moonwalk dash warnings fair and readable? Does his second phase feel fun?
6. With two controllers: check normal/charge/dodge, Start pause/resume, held-button stability. Disconnect a controller during charge: it should cancel, not launch, and the other player keeps control.
7. Win or lose, restart, switch tabs and return. No stuck movement, unexpected charged shot, accumulated damage or leftover boss should appear.

## Reply with

- Hardware/browser/controllers and keyboard rollover issues.
- Is charging worth the time? Is partner boosting understandable?
- Is the boss too easy, too hard, or funny enough?
- Which player or hazard was hard to see?
- One moment that made you laugh.

Local shared-screen only; no online multiplayer or additional map in this update.
