# Snowdown: five-minute couch playtest

Status: **human hardware playtest pending**. Automated keyboard/gamepad simulation does not substitute for this.

Preview: https://game-preview.ninjaprivacy.org/games/snowdown/ (existing sign-in required). Use a desktop browser with hardware acceleration, preferably on the display you will share with friends. Reload after a new build.

## Two humans, one screen

1. Choose **Ride together**. P1 uses WASD/F/G; P2 uses arrows/K/L. Both move and hold throw simultaneously. Check the keyboard does not drop held keys; if it does, try controllers or another keyboard.
2. Walk up and right, then diagonally. Up should look like up-screen. Separate toward opposite arena edges: can each person instantly find their cowboy and read the player marker?
3. Hold throw and dodge in different directions. Can you recognize the windup, snowball release, impact and dodge-ready cue without watching the HUD constantly?
4. Let one partner freeze. Stand beside them to revive. Is the downed player obvious, and does revival feel understandable?
5. Plug in two standard controllers and press a button on each. Check stick movement, A/right-trigger throws, B dodges, Start pause/resume. Hold Start: it should toggle only once. Disconnect one pad; the other player's identity should stay unchanged.
6. Finish or lose a round, restart, then switch to another tab and return. No stuck movement, runaway combat or catch-up burst should occur.

## Reply with

- Hardware/browser/controllers (and keyboard rollover issues, if any).
- Which player was harder to follow?
- What felt confusing or sluggish?
- Was the difficulty too easy, too hard, or fun?
- One moment that made you laugh.

No online multiplayer, charged snowballs, boss or additional map is part of this polish pass.
